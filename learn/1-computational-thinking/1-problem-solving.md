# Problem Solving

## What is Problem Solving?
Problem-solving is the process of finding solutions to complex or challenging situations. It involves identifying, analyzing, and resolving issues systematically.

## Steps in Problem Solving
1. **Understanding the Problem** – Clearly define the problem and gather relevant information.
2. **Breaking Down the Problem** – Divide the problem into smaller, manageable parts.
3. **Exploring Possible Solutions** – Brainstorm different approaches and evaluate their effectiveness.
4. **Implementing the Solution** – Apply the chosen solution and execute the plan.
5. **Reviewing and Refining** – Analyze the outcome and make improvements if necessary.

## Why is it Important?
- Develops critical thinking and decision-making skills.
- Helps in tackling real-world challenges effectively.
- Essential for careers in programming, engineering, business, and more.
